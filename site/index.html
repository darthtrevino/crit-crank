Your 5e character sheet, on a crank.

Crit Crank turns a Playdate into a D&D 5e character sheet. Pick an ability, save, skill or attack, then turn the crank to roll real 3D dice.

Try it

This is the real game, ported to your browser. Drag the crank in a circle (or scroll over the screen) to roll. Click the buttons or use the keys: ↑↓ pick a row, ←→ switch pages, X is Ⓐ (quick-roll), Z is Ⓑ (advantage), [] turn the crank and Esc is Menu.

Bring your own character

charsync is a small command-line tool for Mac, Windows and Linux. It turns a public D&D Beyond character into a Crit Crank hero and sends it to your Playdate over USB.

cd charsync
cargo install --path .
charsync import https://www.dndbeyond.com/characters/12345678

It converts ability scores, saves, skills, HP, AC, spell slots, weapons, damage and healing spells, and limited-use features. Spell and feature descriptions show up on the pause screen and in the Rules viewer too. See the charsync README.

Install

  1. Download crit-crank.pdx.zip.
  2. Sideload it at play.date/account/sideload, or unzip it and copy charsheet.pdx into the Games folder in USB disk mode.
  3. Or build it yourself with the Playdate SDK: cd charsheet && make run.
